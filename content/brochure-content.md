### How Sessions Are Run

- Eyebrow: How Sessions Are Run
- Heading: Programmes built around development.
- Supporting copy: Four principles shape every block, from the first
  fundamentals session through to league readiness.

Principles:

1. **Everyone starts somewhere**
   You do not need competitive experience to enter the ecosystem. The Beginner
   programme assumes nothing except that you want to improve.

2. **Coaching, not just screen time**
   Sessions are structured with a purpose — fundamentals, review, role
   development and match preparation — rather than open-ended play.

3. **Progress you can point at**
   Beginner opens with a four-week foundation phase, and every phase ends with a
   performance assessment — so improvement is visible and the next step is
   obvious.

4. **A route into competition**
   Programmes feed directly into league play and the quarterly tournament
   windows when those launch with the opening programme.

### Tournament Titles

- Eyebrow: Tournament Titles
- Heading: Five titles, three platforms.
- Supporting copy: Console, PC and mobile competition inside a single
  championship programme — each title with its own angle, bracket and running
  order.
- Status: Final line-up planned at launch

Titles:

1. **EA Sports FC 26** — 1v1
   Accessible individual competition and a strong entry point for
   casual-to-competitive progression.
2. **Call of Duty BO6** — 4v4
   Team communication, roles and objective play.
3. **Valorant** — 5v5
   Tactical teamwork and structured competitive preparation.
4. **PUBG Mobile** — Squad
   Mobile community participation and squad coordination.
5. **League of Legends** — 5v5
   Deep team strategy, distinct roles and objective control across a match.

### The Tournament Journey

- Eyebrow: The Tournament Journey
- Heading: From interest to the Arena stage.
- Supporting copy: Every entrant follows the same clear route — no invited
  shortlists, no hidden brackets.
- Status: Dates to be announced · Open & member divisions planned

Stages:

1. **Register interest** — Players, teams, volunteers and media contacts email
   the team — registration opens with the announced dates.
2. **Confirm your entry** — Formats, eligibility and division placement are
   confirmed with the opening programme, with entry fees and closing dates
   published at the same time.
3. **Qualify & compete** — Planned open and member divisions play across the
   five titles, with brackets and running order published ahead of the event.
4. **Finals in the Arena** — The closing matches run on the Arena stage with
   spectators, commentary and coverage — the moment the season builds toward.

### The Wider Rhythm

- Eyebrow: The Wider Rhythm
- Heading: Four tournament windows across the season.
- Supporting copy: The championship is the fourth and final window — the
  season-closing moment the earlier three build toward.

Windows:

- **Window 1** — Open community launch tournament: EA Sports FC 26 and mobile
  showcase.
- **Window 2** — Team competition: Valorant or Call of Duty BO6.
- **Window 3** — Mobile and grassroots tournament: PUBG Mobile and League of
  Legends.
- **Window 4** — Season finals — the Open Esports Championship: The flagship
  championship closes the season on the Arena stage. See the championship page
  for titles, divisions and format.

Publishing caveat: Exact dates, capacity and the final game schedule are still
to be confirmed, and are published with the opening programme.
